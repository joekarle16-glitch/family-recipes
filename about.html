Why this exists

The recipe box, written down before it fades

The cards are getting old, and handwriting doesn't last forever. This is where they live now.

How it works

We're working through the family recipe box, photographing each card and writing it out word for word. Those go up first.

Once the box is done, it's everyone's. Know a recipe by heart? Fill out the recipe form with the dish, who it came from, and how to make it. Sitting on a stack of cards? Drop the photos in the shared folder, as many as you like, and they'll be read and added here.

These recipes are meant to be used: cooked often, spilled on, and handed down.

A note on handwriting

Some of the original cards are hard to read. Where a word or a measurement is uncertain, the recipe says so instead of guessing. If you remember it differently, let us know and we'll set it straight.

The family behind the recipes

Family photos are on their way. If you have one that belongs here, send it along.
