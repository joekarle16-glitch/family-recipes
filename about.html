Why this exists

About this kitchen

The cards in the recipe box are getting old, and handwriting fades. This site keeps them the way they were taught, with credit to the people who taught them.

How it works

Joe and Jim are photographing the family recipe box and writing out each card word for word. Those recipes go up here first.

After that, anyone in the family can add a recipe. Fill out the recipe form with the dish name, who it came from, the ingredients and the steps, and it will be added here. If the family should never lose a recipe, it belongs on this site.

A note on handwriting

Some of the original cards are hard to read. Where a word or a measurement is uncertain, the recipe says so instead of guessing. If you remember it differently, tell Joe or Jim and it will be fixed, with credit to you.

The family behind the recipes

Family photos are on their way. If you have one that belongs here, send it to Joe.
