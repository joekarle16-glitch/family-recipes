Why this exists

About this kitchen

The cards in the recipe box are getting older, and handwriting fades. Writing them down here keeps them exactly as they were taught, with credit to the people who taught them.

How it works

Phase one: Joe and Jim are photographing the family recipe box and transcribing each card word for word. Those recipes appear here first.

Phase two: every family member can add recipes directly. Fill in the recipe form with the dish name, who it came from, the ingredients, and the steps, and it will be added to the site from there. If you have a recipe the family should never lose, it belongs here.

A note on handwriting

Some of the original cards are hard to read. Where a word or a measurement is uncertain, the recipe says so plainly instead of guessing. If you remember it differently, tell Joe or Jim and the recipe will be corrected with credit to you.
