Add a family recipe

Three ways to get a recipe into the collection. Pick whichever is easier.

Drop in card photos

Photograph the cards and drop them in the shared folder, as many as you like. Each one is transcribed automatically, then reviewed and corrected by the family before it goes up.

Open the photo drop folder

Send one with the details

Photos plus the context: your name, whose recipe it is, and any stories that go with it. You can attach up to 10 photos of the card.

Open the recipe form

Write one from memory

No card? Write the recipe out as best you remember, ingredients and steps, and the family will review it before it goes up.

Open the memory form

A few photo tips

Snap a clear photo of each card, front and back if needed. Good light and a straight-on angle help a lot.

If you have a photo of the finished dish, send it too. It could become the recipe's photo.

Don't worry about order or naming the files. Just get the photos in and we'll sort them out.

What happens next

Once your photos are in, they're transcribed and checked by the family. Want to fix something on a recipe that's already up instead? Open it and hit "Suggest a correction" there.
