Add a family recipe

Two ways to get a recipe into the collection. Pick whichever is easier.

Drop in card photos

Photograph the cards and drop them in the shared folder, as many as you like. Each one is transcribed automatically, then reviewed and corrected by the family before it goes up.

Open the photo drop folder

Type it out yourself

Rather write it out? The form walks you through it: the recipe title, whose it is, how to make it, and any stories that go with it. You can attach up to 10 photos of the card.

Open the recipe form

A few photo tips

Snap a clear photo of each card, front and back if needed. Good light and a straight-on angle help a lot.

If you have a photo of the finished dish, send it too. It could become the recipe's photo.

Don't worry about order or naming the files. Just get the photos in and we'll sort them out.

What happens next

Once your photos are in, they're transcribed and checked by the family. Spot something off after a recipe goes up? Every recipe has a "Suggest a correction" button. Send the fix and we'll update the site.
